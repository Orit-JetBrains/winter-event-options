# Option 5 — Tel Aviv: Interactive Kosher Cooking / Cocktail Workshop 🍴🍸

![Tel Aviv](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/69/Sarona_CBD_01_%28cropped%29.jpg/960px-Sarona_CBD_01_%28cropped%29.jpg)

*The easy, central, all-in-one fun option — a hands-on kosher cooking or cocktail workshop in the heart of Tel Aviv. (No yacht! 🚫⛵)*

## The day
Meet in central Tel Aviv for a **hands-on team workshop at The Cooking Studio (Bishulon)** — cook together (or mix cocktails), then sit down to the full meal you made, with drinks. Indoor, weather-proof, and the activity + meal + drinks are all one booking.

## Highlights
- 🔪 **"Knife Fight"** or **Classic Cooking Class** — hands-on, chef-guided, competitive & fun
- 🛒 **"Shuk & Cook"** — split into teams, shop the Tel Aviv market with the chefs, then cook
- 🍸 **Cocktail Class** — mix-your-own with a bartender
- 🍽️ Finish with a **rich 7–10 course meal**, unlimited **wine & beer** included

## Kosher meal ✅ (built in)
- **The Cooking Studio (Bishulon)** — under **Tel Aviv Rabbinate** supervision; handles team days (up to 250). The meal *is* the activity — everything kosher, including the wine & beer.
- (Alt: **Foodsteps** — kosher cooking school, private classes in English.)

## Logistics
- **Transport:** ✅ **Best in the country** — central Tel Aviv, train + buses, easy for everyone.
- **Weekday:** fully flexible (indoor).
- **Cost:** private team events **from ~280 NIS + VAT pp** (~330 NIS / €85 all-in incl. meal + drinks) → **well within ~€130 pp**, with margin.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Single venue, fully kosher, activity + full meal + drinks in one price; central & weather-proof; genuinely fun/interactive.
- ❌ Indoor (not a "day out in nature"); less of a destination than Akko/Zichron.
