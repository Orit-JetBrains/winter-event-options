# Option 3 — Zichron Yaakov 🍷🥩

![Zichron Yaakov](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b7/PikiWiki_Israel_73640_glider_photo_of_zichron_yaacov.jpg/960px-PikiWiki_Israel_73640_glider_photo_of_zichron_yaacov.jpg)

*A charming hilltop wine-country town (founded 1882) — a pretty pedestrian street, First-Aliyah history, wineries, and a genuinely good chef kosher meat restaurant.*

## The day
Train to **Binyamina** → **bus 70** (~7 min) up to Zichron → wander the **Midrachov** (pedestrian street), museums & synagogue → **kosher meat lunch at HaMeyasdim 16** → optional **winery tour & tasting** (Carmel Winery in town, or Tishbi/Binyamina on the way).

## Highlights
- 🚶 **Midrachov (HaMeyasdim St)** — restored 1882 pioneer houses, cafés, boutiques
- 🏛️ **First Aliyah Museum** · **Nili / Aaronsohn House** (WWI spy-ring story)
- 🕍 **Ohel Ya'akov Synagogue** (1886, iconic clock tower)
- 🍇 **Carmel Winery** (Israel's oldest) tour & tasting; 🌿 **Ramat HaNadiv** Rothschild gardens
- ➕ Optional **Tishbi / Binyamina winery** tasting en route (~55 NIS pp, Sun–Thu)

## Kosher meal ✅ (meat!)
- **HaMeyasdim 16 / "The Founders 16"** — family-run **kosher meat** restaurant by chef Alon Pingold, right on the Midrachov, **sea-view covered & heated terrace** (great for November), wood taboun oven, fresh focaccia. **Sun–Wed 12:00–21:00, Thu 12:00–22:00** (closed Fri–Sat).
- (Also: *Batya & Nachman* smoked-meat grill; *Derech HaYayin* meat.)

## Logistics
- **Transport:** ⚠️ One transfer — no train station of its own. **Train → Binyamina**, then **bus 70** (~every 20 min) or a short taxi. ⚠️ On the return, not all trains stop at Binyamina — check times.
- **Weekday:** Sun–Thu (restaurant closed Fri–Sat).
- **Cost:** museums/winery 35–65 NIS + meal → within ~520 NIS (€130) pp.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Charming town + **wine-country** angle + a strong **kosher meat** restaurant with sea views.
- ❌ One bus transfer; check return train times at Binyamina.
