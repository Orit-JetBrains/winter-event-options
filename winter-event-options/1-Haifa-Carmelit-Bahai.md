# Option 1 — Haifa: Carmelit + Baha'i Gardens 🌿

![Baha'i Gardens, Haifa](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6e/TerracesBenGurion2.jpg/960px-TerracesBenGurion2.jpg)

*Israel's only subway (the Carmelit funicular) + the UNESCO Baha'i Gardens, finishing in the German Colony.*

## The day
Train to Haifa → ride the **Carmelit** funicular up Mount Carmel → **Louis Promenade** viewpoint → **free guided Baha'i Gardens tour (English, 10:00)** which descends through the terraces (incl. the Shrine of the Báb, open mornings only) → stroll the **German Colony** → lunch → afternoon free in the **port / Turkish Market (Compound 21) / Wadi Nisnas** (street art, galleries).

## Highlights
- 🚇 **Carmelit** — quirky underground funicular, 6 stops, ~8 min, 5.90 NIS
- 🌸 **Baha'i Gardens** guided tour — free, 50 min, 750 steps **downhill**
- 🏙️ **German Colony** — restored 19th-c Templer houses on Ben-Gurion Blvd
- 🎨 **Downtown / port** — revitalised Turkish Market, street art, museums

## Kosher meal ⚠️
- **Terrace (טרסה)** — kosher-**dairy** chef restaurant (opened Aug 2025) in the German Colony, meat-free but strong veg/vegan; Mediterranean, bay views.
- ⚠️ **No good sit-down kosher *meat* restaurant** near the gardens (El Gaucho closed; Ruben is fast-food). Best fit only if we're happy with **dairy**.

## Logistics
- **Transport:** ✅ Easy — train + Carmelit.
- **Weekday:** Sun / Tue / Wed / Thu (gardens **closed Monday**).
- **Cost:** Tour **free** + Carmelit ~6 NIS → almost the whole ~520 NIS (€130) pp budget goes to the meal.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Unique, iconic, very cheap activity, excellent transport.
- ❌ Weak on a *quality kosher meat* meal; 750 steps (not step-free).
