# Option 2 — Akko (Acre) Old City ⚓

![Akko old city and fishing port](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4e/Acre_-_Akko_6_-_the_fishing_port_%286658890981%29.jpg/960px-Acre_-_Akko_6_-_the_fishing_port_%286658890981%29.jpg)

*A compact, walkable UNESCO World Heritage old city by the sea — Crusader halls, bazaars, and a proper kosher meat restaurant inside the fortress.*

## The day
Direct train to Akko → short bus (3A/4) or taxi into the Old City → explore the Crusader old city (guided tour optional) → **kosher meat lunch at Roots** in the Knights' Halls courtyard → sea-wall & port walk.

## Highlights
- 🏰 **Hospitaller Fortress / Knights' Halls** — vast Crusader halls
- 🕳️ **Templar Tunnel** — underground passage to the port
- 🕌 **Al-Jazzar Mosque**, **Turkish Bazaar**, **Khan al-Umdan**, **Hammam al-Basha**
- 🌊 **Sea walls & fishing port** — great seaside walk
- 🎟️ Usually sold as an **"Enchanted Akko" combo ticket**; easy to add a guided tour

## Kosher meal ✅ (meat!)
- **Roots** — highly-regarded **kosher meat** restaurant set in the **Knights' Halls courtyard** (ancient fortress, stone arches). Levantine menu with house-made pareve cheeses. **Sun–Thu 12:00–23:00**. **Book ahead.**
- (Also: *Aamasbia* — kosher dairy in the Turkish Bazaar, if wanted.)

## Logistics
- **Transport:** ✅ Direct train (Tel Aviv ~1h40, Haifa ~30 min) + **bus 3A/4** (~10 min, ~5 NIS) or taxi ~20 NIS. Rav-Kav / Moovit.
- **Weekday:** any weekday (Roots closed Fri–Sat).
- **Cost:** combo ticket + meal → comfortably within ~520 NIS (€130) pp.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Best transport **and** an atmospheric sit-down **kosher meat** meal; compact & walkable.
- ❌ Most other old-city food is non-kosher (fine — Roots covers us).
