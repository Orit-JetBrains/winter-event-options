# Option 8 — Jerusalem: Machane Yehuda (the Shuk) 🥘

![Mahane Yehuda Market](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5b/Mahane_Yehuda_%28I%29_%2845298221191%29.jpg/960px-Mahane_Yehuda_%28I%29_%2845298221191%29.jpg)

*Food-forward and kosher-rich — a hands-on chef workshop or tasting tour in Jerusalem's famous market. Possibly the best-balanced option after Akko.*

## The day
Fast train to Jerusalem → light rail to **Machane Yehuda** → **chef-led cooking workshop** (shop the shuk, then cook & eat) or a **kosher tasting tour** → optional Old City / Tower of David in the afternoon.

## Highlights
- 👨‍🍳 **Cooking workshop** — shop the market with a chef, then cook a multi-course meal
- 🧆 **Kosher tasting tour** — curated bites across certified vendors (hummus, halva, rugelach…)
- 🍷 The shuk's lively bar/café scene by day; 🏛️ add the **Old City** or **Tower of David** show

## Kosher meal ✅✅ (abundant)
- **iTravelJerusalem** market tour + cooking class ~**$90 pp** (vegan version ~$75 pp).
- **Harry's Baked** — fully **kosher (Mehadrin)** food tour, ~**$130 pp**.
- **Giraffa** kosher market tours from **₪225 pp** (groups up to 20).
- Jerusalem is wall-to-wall kosher, so the meal is the easiest of any option.

## Logistics
- **Transport:** ✅✅ **Fast train from Tel Aviv (~30–35 min)** to Jerusalem-Yitzhak Navon + light rail to the shuk. Excellent for an Israel-wide team.
- **Weekday:** Sun–Thu (tours don't run Fri/Sat).
- **Cost:** ~₪280–480 pp → within ~€130 pp.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Ticks everything — weekday, kosher ✅✅, superb transport, fun & food-forward.
- ❌ The shuk is busy/crowded (part of the charm); book the workshop ahead.
