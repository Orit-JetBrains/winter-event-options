# Option 4 — Emek HaEla: Off-Road Rangers + Winery / Beer 🚜🍷🍺

![Valley of Elah](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9e/Valley_of_Elah_from_Tel_Azeka.jpg/960px-Valley_of_Elah_from_Tel_Azeka.jpg)

*The most active / adventurous option — off-road ranger/jeep tour in the David-vs-Goliath valley, paired with wine (or craft beer) tasting. 🚗 We'd drive here by car.*

## The day
Drive to the **Elah Valley (Emek HaEla)** → **off-road ranger / jeep tour** (team-building + biblical-landscape scenery) → **winery tour & tasting** and/or **craft-brewery beer garden** → optional group meal.

## Highlights
- 🚜 **Off-road adventure** — ranger/jeep tour through the Judean Hills & vineyards (Hidden Valley Tours runs jeep tours here and can add winery visits / ODT team-building)
- 🍷 **Wineries (kosher):** **Ella Valley Winery** (largest, kosher), **Tzora Vineyards** (kosher, top terroir), **Teperberg** (historic, kosher) — tours + tastings
- 🍺 **Srigim Brewery** — boutique craft brewery with a **beer garden** overlooking the valley (⚠️ the **brewery is not kosher** — but it's ringed by kosher cafés, below)
- 📖 Bonus: the valley of the **David & Goliath** story; optional Khirbet Qeiyafa fortress

## Kosher food ✅ (yes — the wineries serve food too)
- 🍷 **Ella Valley Winery** (kosher, Badatz, pareve) — **Friday food menu till 4pm with live music**; **takeaway picnics** (wine, cheese, salads) for groups any day. Sun–Thu 9:00–16:00.
- 🧀 **Tzora Vineyards** (kosher, dairy) — tastings + cheese pairings.
- 🍔 **Burger Road Srigim** (kosher **meat**, Badatz Beit Yosef) — burgers & shared plates, at the Srigim complex (right by the brewery).
- 🍝 **Café Li-On** (kosher **mehadrin dairy**) — Tuscan-style sit-down with valley views, next to the brewery. Sun–Thu 10:00–23:00.
- 🥩 **Hans Sternbach Winery** (Givat Yeshayahu) — winery + **meat / charcuterie** restaurant (verify current kashrut).
- ⚠️ Beer note: since the brewery isn't kosher, lean on the **kosher wineries** for the drink/tasting, or ask about a kosher-beer alternative. Also confirm whether the off-road operator uses **RZR/Ranger** vehicles (they advertise jeeps).

## Logistics
- **Transport:** 🚗 **By car** (team agreed to drive) — this is the one option that doesn't rely on public transport.
- **Weekday:** winery tastings Sun–Thu; book tours ahead.
- **Cost (ranger estimate):** off-road runs **per vehicle** — e.g. a **1–2 hr** 4-seat ATV ≈ **175–275 NIS pp**, a 5-seat jeep ≈ **200–300 NIS pp**; branded **3–4 hr guided ATV/jeep adventures ≈ $125–130 pp (~480 NIS)**; premium self-drive **RZR/Can-Am half-days are the most expensive** (€100+ pp). Wine/chocolate tasting ~55 NIS pp.
- ⚖️ **Viability:** works within **~€130 pp only if the ride is kept short (~1–2 hrs)**, leaving room for tasting + a kosher meal. A **half-day RZR adventure + winery + meal would exceed €130 pp** — so pick a short off-road session, or treat this as a higher-budget option. (Exact quote still worth getting from the operator.)

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ The most **fun / active** option; adventure **plus** tasting; flexible by car.
- ❌ Needs cars & a designated driver (alcohol); a few things to confirm (RZR vs jeep, beer, kosher food, full cost).
