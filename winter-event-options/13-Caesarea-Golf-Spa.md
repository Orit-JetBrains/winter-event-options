# Option 13 — Caesarea: Archery & Shooting (or Golf / Spa) 🏹🎯

![Caesarea](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a9/Caesarea.JPG/960px-Caesarea.JPG)

*An active seaside day — archery & clay-pigeon shooting + team-building, with golf OR spa as a premium upgrade. 🚗 By car.*

## The day
Drive to **Caesarea** → **archery + target / clay-pigeon shooting + team-building** at the **AGM / "Danny High"** activity complex → kosher lunch on the harbour → optional ruins/harbour stroll. *(Or swap the activities for a premium **golf** round **or** **spa** day — pick one, not both.)*

## Highlights
- 🏹 **Archery** + 🎯 **target & clay-pigeon ("plate") shooting** + team-building — at the **AGM / "Danny High"** complex (licensed range; also paintball, obstacle course, jeeps)
- 🏺 Optional **pottery/clay workshop** (via the golf club's events)
- ⛳ **OR** a round at **Caesarea Golf Club** (Israel's only 18-hole) — *premium*
- 💆 **OR** a **spa** day at the **Dan Caesarea** resort — *premium*

## Kosher meal ✅
- **The Crusaders (Hatzel-Banim)** — kosher meat+fish on the harbour; or kosher dining at the Dan Caesarea resort.

## Logistics
- **Transport:** 🚗 By car (~45 min from Tel Aviv / 40 min from Haifa).
- **Weekday:** Sun–Thu, by prior arrangement — **book ahead** (and tee times/spa if going premium).
- **Cost:**
  - ✅ **Archery + clay-shooting team-building ≈ ₪199 pp** (+VAT; pricing by quote) → **within ~€130 pp**.
  - ⚠️ **Golf OR spa = premium:** green fees **~₪480–540 pp** (+buggy) — over budget on their own, so **pick one, not both**, and it needs **extra approval**.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Flexible: a **budget-friendly active day** (archery/shooting) *or* a premium golf/spa treat; seaside, fun and competitive.
- ❌ By car; golf/spa exceed budget (choose one, extra approval); activity-complex pricing is by quote.
