# Option 13 — Caesarea: Golf & Spa Day ⛳💆 (premium)

![Caesarea](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a9/Caesarea.JPG/960px-Caesarea.JPG)

*A premium, relaxed treat — a round at Israel's only 18-hole golf course + spa by the Mediterranean. 🚗 By car. ⚠️ Likely over the €130 budget.*

## The day
Drive to **Caesarea** → **golf at the Caesarea Golf Club** (round, or a beginner group lesson / driving-range + buggy tour) → **spa** at the adjacent **Dan Caesarea** resort → kosher lunch on the harbour.

## Highlights
- ⛳ **Caesarea Golf Club** — Israel's **only 18-hole championship course** (Pete Dye redesign, Rolex World Top-1000); caters to groups, lessons & beginners
- 💆 **Dan Caesarea** resort — spa, pool, landscaped gardens
- 🌊 Finish with the harbour / ruins nearby if time allows

## Kosher meal ✅
- **The Crusaders (Hatzel-Banim)** — kosher meat+fish on the harbour; or kosher dining at the Dan Caesarea resort.

## Logistics
- **Transport:** 🚗 By car (~45 min from Tel Aviv / 40 min from Haifa).
- **Weekday:** golf tee times should be **pre-booked** (especially mornings); spa by appointment.
- **Cost:** ⚠️ **Over budget.** Green fees **~₪480–540 pp** (buggy ~₪130 extra) — that alone is roughly the whole €130 cap — plus a **spa day pass** on top. **Would need extra budget approval.**

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Unique premium treat (golf is rare in Israel), relaxing, scenic; great for a "reward" day.
- ❌ **Exceeds the ~€130 pp budget**; by car; golf suits a mixed-ability group best via a lesson/driving-range format.
