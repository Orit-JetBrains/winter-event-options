# Option 7 — Caesarea 🏛️🌊

![Caesarea](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a9/Caesarea.JPG/960px-Caesarea.JPG)

*Stunning seaside Roman & Crusader ruins on the Mediterranean, with kosher harbour dining right by the water. 🚗 We'd drive (easy by car).*

## The day
Drive to **Caesarea National Park** → explore the Roman theatre, Herod's palace & harbour, the Crusader fortress → **kosher lunch on the harbour** → optional guided tour / aqueduct beach walk.

## Highlights
- 🏟️ **Roman theatre** (still used for concerts) & the **hippodrome**
- 🏛️ **Herod's promontory palace** jutting into the sea
- 🏰 **Crusader fortress & port**, moat and gate
- 🌊 **Aqueduct Beach** — the photogenic Roman aqueduct on the sand
- 🖼️ Harbour **shops, galleries** + an optional guided tour / multimedia "Caesarea Experience"

## Kosher meal ✅ (meat + fish, harbour view)
- **The Crusaders Restaurant (Hatzel-Banim)** — kosher **meat & fish**, **Badatz Beit Yosef**, 55+ years on the ancient harbour with the **best sea views**; Sun–Thu ~11:00–23:00. (Dairy alternatives on the harbour: **Aresto** and **Port Café**.)
- Note: restaurants are **inside the National Park** (park admission applies — usually discounted if you're dining).

## Logistics
- **Transport:** 🚗 **By car** — easy (~45 min from Tel Aviv, ~40 min from Haifa). ⚠️ By public transport it's awkward: train to **Binyamina** + the **infrequent (~hourly) bus #80** — so car is the way.
- **Weekday:** park open daily; good any weekday.
- **Cost:** park entry (~₪40–50) + kosher meal → within ~€130 pp.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Spectacular **seaside ruins** + a kosher **meat/fish** meal with harbour views; easy & quick **by car**; pleasant coastal November weather.
- ❌ Relies on **cars** (poor public transport); the core activity is "wander the ruins" unless you add a guided tour or harbour diving/snorkel (November water is cool).
