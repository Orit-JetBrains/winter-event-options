# Option 6 — Emek HaHula: Golf Carts + Crane Migration 🛺🦢

![Agamon HaHula](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/88/View_of_Agamon_Hula.JPG/960px-View_of_Agamon_Hula.JPG)

*Tour the Hula Lake Park by golf cart during November's spectacular crane migration — tens of thousands of birds. 🚗 We'd drive north.*

## The day
Drive up to **Agamon HaHula (Hula Lake Park)** → rent **golf carts** and loop the circular route past the wetlands and **migrating cranes** → visitor-center break → optional kosher meal in the Upper Galilee.

## Highlights
- 🛺 **Golf-cart tour** of the park loop (also bikes / pedal carts available)
- 🦢 **Peak crane migration** — ~35,000 cranes arrive in autumn; **November is prime time**
- 🌅 Most magical at **sunrise / sunset** (when the flocks land & take off)
- 🌿 Beautiful flat wetland scenery, easygoing and photogenic

## Kosher food ✅ (plenty — book ahead for ~7)
**Kiryat Shmona** (closest, ~15 min):
- **Amigos** — kosher *mehadrin* meat grill, 20+ yrs, highly rated, with a terrace. (Also *Esh Besh Asado & Grill*, *Mama Halsa*, *Shipudei Ron*.)

**Rosh Pina** (~15–20 min, more scenic):
- **Pina BaRosh** — *mehadrin* **chef** restaurant with a **phenomenal view over the Hula Valley to Mt Hermon** (meat lunch/dinner; separate dairy-breakfast kitchen).
- **Eretz Canaan** — kosher chef restaurant, authentic Galilee cuisine, **handles groups up to 150**; Sun–Thu 14:00–22:00.
- **Meatbalim** — well-known kosher meat (Rabbanut), right at the Rosh Pina highway entrance — convenient for a group.

**On the Jordan River:** **Iskander** — kosher meat on the riverbank, runs team-building/fun days (can combine with rafting).

*(The park's own visitor-center cafeteria — confirm kashrut if you'd rather eat on-site.)*

## Logistics
- **Transport:** 🚗 **By car** — Hula is in the far **Upper Galilee** (~2.5–3 hrs from the center). Longest travel of all options.
- **Weekday:** fine; aim for a **dawn or late-afternoon** slot for the birds. Book carts ahead in peak season (they sell out).
- **Cost:** golf cart **149 / 199 / 259 NIS** for 2 / 4 / 6 people (75 min) → **~60 NIS pp** + park entry + meal → easily within ~€130 pp.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Unique **seasonal wildlife spectacle**, fun golf carts, very cheap activity.
- ❌ **Long drive** north (needs cars + an early start for best birds).
