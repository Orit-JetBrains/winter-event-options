# Option 9 — Jaffa (Tel Aviv-Yafo): Old City + Flea Market 🧭

![Old Jaffa](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a3/ISR-2013-Aerial-Jaffa-Port_of_Jaffa.jpg/960px-ISR-2013-Aerial-Jaffa-Port_of_Jaffa.jpg)

*Atmospheric seaside old city + the buzzing flea market — central, superb transport, easygoing wander with a kosher meal.*

## The day
Train to Tel Aviv → light rail/bus to **Jaffa** → wander **Old Jaffa's** stone alleys, the port and the **Clock Tower** → explore the **Shuk HaPishpeshim (flea market)** → kosher lunch → optional guided or food tour.

## Highlights
- 🕌 **Old Jaffa** — alleys, artists' quarter, port, Clock Tower, sea views
- 🛍️ **Flea market (Shuk HaPishpeshim)** — antiques, design, cafés, lively **Sun–Thu**
- 🎨 Galleries + optional guided history/food tour

## Kosher meal ✅ (choose certified spots)
- **Hummus Danny** — "best kosher hummus bar," in the flea market.
- **Goshen** — informal kosher, meat/fish/burgers.
- **Uno** — kosher Italian. **The Market House Hotel** has a kosher restaurant right by the market.
- ⚠️ Many famous Jaffa spots (Puaa, Abu Hassan, Onza…) are **not kosher** — stick to the certified ones above.

## Logistics
- **Transport:** ✅✅ Central Tel Aviv — train + **Red Line light rail** (or bus) straight to Jaffa. Easiest in the country.
- **Weekday:** Sun–Thu (flea market liveliest; avoid Fri crowds).
- **Cost:** tour + kosher meal → within ~€130 pp.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Central, atmospheric, superb transport, flexible; easy half-day + meal.
- ❌ Strictly-kosher diners must pick certified venues; it's a "wander" rather than a structured activity (add a tour if you want more).
