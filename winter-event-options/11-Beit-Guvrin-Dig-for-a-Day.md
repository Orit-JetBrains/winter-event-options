# Option 11 — Beit Guvrin: "Dig for a Day" 🏺⛏️

![Beit Guvrin bell cave](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6f/Beit_Guvrin_1.JPG/960px-Beit_Guvrin_1.JPG)

*The most unique, hands-on team activity — take part in a real archaeological dig in the ancient caves. 🚗 By car.*

## The day
Drive to **Beit Guvrin-Maresha National Park** → join an **authentic archaeological dig** (licensed by the Israel Antiquities Authority) — excavate, sift and explore 2,000-year-old caves → optional restoration studio or guided park tour → kosher meal nearby (Elah Valley is ~15 min away).

## Highlights
- ⛏️ **Real dig** — ~2.5–3 hrs, you actually excavate & handle finds (guided, English/Hebrew)
- 🕳️ Explore the famous **bell caves** and cave complexes
- 🏺 Memorable, genuinely different team experience; great for groups

## Kosher food ⚠️ (arrange nearby)
- On-site is limited (gas-station store / snack stand) — **not** a meal.
- Nearby **Elah Valley (Srigim, ~15 min):** **Burger Road** (kosher meat, Badatz Beit Yosef) or **Café Li-On** (kosher dairy). Also kosher options in **Kiryat Gat**. Book ahead; confirm kashrut.

## Logistics
- **Transport:** 🚗 **By car** — there's **no public transport** to Beit Guvrin (and no taxis on site), so arrange the round trip. ~1 hr from Tel Aviv.
- **Weekday:** Sun–Fri (park hours); **book the dig in advance** (digforaday.com).
- **Cost:** dig **₪95 pp** (private group up to 12 ≈ ₪1,560; up to 20 ≈ ₪2,640) + park entry **₪28 pp** → very cheap, leaves plenty for the meal.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Unique, hands-on and memorable; inexpensive; pairs with Elah Valley kosher food.
- ❌ By car only; dusty/physical; kosher meal is a separate nearby booking.
