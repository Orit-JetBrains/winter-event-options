# Option 12 — Ein Gedi + Dead Sea 🏜️🧂

![David Falls, Ein Gedi](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a7/2010-06-18_09-30-17_Israel_Ejn_Gedi_JH_%2852783702009%29.jpg/960px-2010-06-18_09-30-17_Israel_Ejn_Gedi_JH_%2852783702009%29.jpg)

*Desert oasis + a Dead Sea float — a relaxing, photogenic "experience" day. Warm in November. 🚗 By car.*

## The day
Drive down to **Ein Gedi Nature Reserve** → walk the oasis trails (waterfalls, springs, ibex & hyrax) → **kosher group lunch** → a **Dead Sea float / spa** → head back.

## Highlights
- 💧 **Ein Gedi Nature Reserve** — David Falls, pools, desert wildlife
- 🧖 **Dead Sea float** + optional spa / mud treatments
- ☀️ **Warm in November** — one of the best winter-weather picks

## Kosher food ✅
- **Kibbutz Ein Gedi Hotel** dining room — **kosher buffet**, well-suited to groups (keeps kosher year-round).
- **Baobar** (kibbutz lobby bar) — kosher **dairy** café on a deck in the botanical gardens.
- **Asado BaMidbar** — kosher **meat** food truck overlooking the Dead Sea (Avnat complex).

## Logistics
- **Transport:** 🚗 **By car** (Road 90). Public transport is sparse/long, so cars are the way. ~2 hrs from Tel Aviv / ~1.5 hrs from Jerusalem.
- **Weekday:** reserve open **Oct–Mar 08:00–16:00** → start **early** (gates close 4pm in November).
- **Cost:** reserve group rate ~**₪26 pp** + kosher meal → within ~€130 pp.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Unique nature + water experience, relaxing, warm November weather, strong kosher dining on the kibbutz.
- ❌ Long drive; early start needed (reserve closes 4pm); more "relaxed day out" than high-energy team activity.
