# Option 10 — Tel Aviv: Florentin Graffiti / Street-Art Workshop 🎨

![Florentin, Tel Aviv](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3e/Florentin0013.JPG/960px-Florentin0013.JPG)

*The creative, hands-on fun option — a guided street-art walk through Florentin, then spray your own piece. Central and lively.*

## The day
Meet in **Florentin** → guided **street-art / graffiti tour** of the murals → **hands-on spray-paint workshop** (create a group/solo piece with a master artist) → kosher meal nearby.

## Highlights
- 🖌️ **Graffiti workshop** with a street artist — learn techniques, then paint; keep a souvenir
- 🧱 **Florentin** — Tel Aviv's edgy street-art quarter, murals & cafés
- 🤝 Great team energy; creative and memorable

## Kosher meal ✅
- Plenty of **kosher-certified** restaurants/cafés in central Tel Aviv — pick one near Florentin and pre-book for ~7 (confirm the venue's certificate).

## Logistics
- **Transport:** ✅✅ Central Tel Aviv — train + bus/light rail. Easy for everyone.
- **Weekday:** any weekday; the walking portion is outdoors (November is usually fine).
- **Cost:** workshop **~300 NIS pp** (groups 8–60, discount for 10+); or a tour + spray-paint add-on ~**₪210 pp**. Plus the meal → within ~€130 pp.

## Pros / Cons
- ✅ Fun, creative, hands-on; central & transport-easy; weekday-flexible.
- ❌ Outdoor walking segment (weather-dependent); meal is a separate booking (easy — pick a kosher spot nearby).
